SATYR ARCANA I

What Is the Satyr Arcana?

A living mythology — a full 78-card tarot released across six albums, where every card becomes a track and an original piece of art. The artwork is portrait-based: every card is someone he loves — chosen family, ancestors, lovers, friends. Some are still here. Some have crossed over. The deck doesn't separate them — it bridges the living, the dead, and the supernatural, celebrating the life and love they shared while they walked this same world together.

Not The Fool. The Dream Traveler.

Why the deck starts here

Most tarot decks open with the Fool — a blank slate, the beginning of someone else's story. The Satyr Arcana opens with Card I, the Dream Traveler, because this deck was never a blank slate. It's his own journey, already in motion, already carrying the people he loves with him. The Dream Traveler walks between worlds — the living, the dead, the club floor, the astral one — because that's the vantage point the whole deck is told from.
